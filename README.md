## 🏆 Build Award

This robot received the official FIRST LEGO League **Build Award**, which recognises superior mechanical design, structural integrity, and thoughtful engineering — independent of how well the robot performed in a match.

Judging for the Build Award focuses on:

- **Structural durability** — does the robot survive a full competition season of repeated impact?
- **Sound engineering** — are materials and connections used appropriately?
- **Thoughtful design** — is the mechanism elegant, or over-engineered?

## Design highlights

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Power transmission** | High-torque gear ratio optimised for precise, repeatable mission delivery |
| **Modularity** | Quick-swap attachment mechanisms so the robot retools between missions in seconds |
| **Structure** | Load paths designed for durability across hundreds of competition runs |

## Season

**FLL Unearthed** — the season centred on archaeology, excavation, and uncovering artefacts.

## Why the Build Award mattered

Robot design is usually judged on performance. Winning the Build Award means the *engineering itself* was judged to be excellent — the mechanics are sound, not just effective in one specific match configuration.
